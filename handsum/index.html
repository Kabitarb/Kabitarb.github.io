HandSum

Point the camera at columns of handwritten numbers. Each column's total appears under its last number, live.

  • Write one number per line, columns clearly apart.
  • Circle a number to subtract it.
  • Decimals: a clear dot, e.g. 12.5.
  • Hold the phone still for a second; AI mode updates every ~2 s.
